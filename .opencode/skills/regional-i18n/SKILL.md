## Localization Checklist
- Ensure Google Font fallbacks for Devanagari, Tamil, Telugu, and Bengali scripts.
- Validate dynamic translation key interpolation without layout clipping.
- Inject localized `<link rel="alternate" hreflang="..." />` tags into head elements.
